== Description ==

The Consultancy Firm Theme is a professional, multipurpose, and modern solution designed for consulting businesses including business consultancy, management consulting, financial consulting, IT consulting, HR consulting, legal consulting, tax consulting, real estate consulting, SME consulting, staffing consultancy, recruitment agencies, and advising attorney services, making it ideal for consulting agency and corporation-level firms as well as financial analysts offering expert solutions. With a sleek, minimal, and intuitive layout, it offers full customization for corporate and personal branding, supporting services such as corporate strategy, business growth, market research, risk management, project management, sales consulting, digital consulting, organizational development, investment consulting, leadership training, HR solutions, and change management. The responsive, mobile-friendly design ensures smooth navigation on desktops, tablets, and smartphones, while optimized and secure code guarantees fast loading and reliable performance. Interactive banners, testimonial sections, strategically placed call-to-action buttons, and social media integration enhance user engagement, credibility, and conversions. Built on the Bootstrap framework with easy-to-use shortcodes, the theme is translation-ready, SEO-friendly, and fully compatible with WooCommerce for service payments and Contact Form 7 for lead generation, along with support for tools like the WPForms plugin to streamline client inquiries, helping consulting firms build a professional, high-performing, and globally accessible online presence that drives long-term growth.

== Installation ==

1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance > Themes and click the Add New button.
2. Click Upload and Choose File, then select the theme's .zip file. Click Install Now.
3. Click Activate to use your new theme right away.
